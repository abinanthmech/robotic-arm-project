# Test Results

## Servo Functional Test

### Objective
To verify the basic operation and angular movement of the servo motor.

### Test
The servo was connected to the ESP32 and commanded to move progressively
between approximately 0° and 180°.

### Expected Result
The servo should move smoothly through the commanded angular range without
unexpected stopping or uncontrolled movement.

### Result
The servo movement was successfully demonstrated during the initial
functional test.

### Evidence
A video recording of the servo functional test is included in the project
documentation.

## Notes

This test was performed as an individual servo/component test before the
complete robotic arm assembly.
